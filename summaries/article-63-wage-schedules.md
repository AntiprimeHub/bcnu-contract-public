# Article 63 – Wage Schedules — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-63-wage-schedules.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-63-wage-schedules-a-1-general-wage-increases.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 3 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 2 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **3% / 3% / 3% / 3% General Wage Increase (≈12.5% compounded over 4 years)** — guaranteed across-the-board GWI on every level and step, effective the first pay period after **April 1 2025, April 1 2026, April 1 2027, April 1 2028**. This is a brand-new 4-year wage ladder replacing the expired 2022–2024 schedule. *(A.1, PDF p.116)*
- **New "Correctional Health Services" premium** — nurses working in Correctional Health Services get a **2%** premium (of the Level 3, Fourth-Year hourly rate), effective first pay period after **April 1 2026** (deferred). The A.2 premium heading is also expanded to "Forensic and Corrections." *(A.2, PDF p.115)*
- **Existing Forensic premiums carried forward** — Forensic Nurse A keeps 4% (Level 3, 8th-yr rate); Forensic Nurse B / FCLNs keep 2% (Level 3, 4th-yr rate). No reduction. *(A.2, PDF p.115)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- The full wage-rate grids (Levels 1–6 plus Pine Free Clinic, all four years, PDF p.117–132) carry **no tracked-change marks** — they are the clean tables produced by applying the 3% GWIs above. Nothing to itemize; the entitlement is the GWI %s and dates, not individual cells.

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **GWIs are partly retroactive, partly deferred.** Dated 2025/2026/2027/2028 with the deal signed May 2026 — Year 1 (Apr 2025) and Year 2 (Apr 2026) are effectively retroactive on ratification, while **Year 3 (Apr 2027) and Year 4 (Apr 2028) are future/deferred**. Nurses don't see those last two raises until those dates arrive. *(A.1, PDF p.116)*
- **Old COLA top-up is gone from the wage formula.** The struck 2022–2024 language included "additional GWI of up to 1.25% / up to 1%" COLA riders; the new 3%/yr GWIs are fixed with **no COLA rider** attached. Note: those riders only ever applied to April 2023/2024 (already spent — see the appendix-vv summary), so this is **not a forward-looking loss** — it just means the 2025–2029 term has fixed raises and no CPI-triggered top-up. *(A.1 struck text, PDF p.116)*
