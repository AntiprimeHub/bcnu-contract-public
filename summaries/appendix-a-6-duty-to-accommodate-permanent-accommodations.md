# Appendix A.6 – Duty to Accommodate, Permanent Accommodations — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/appendix-a-6-duty-to-accommodate-permanent-accommodations.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/appendix-a-6-memorandum-of-agreement-duty-to-accommodate-permanent-accomm.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **3-week medical review deadline** — Employer must, within **three (3) weeks** of receiving an employee's medical, either accept it or state (with rationale) what more is needed. Hard timeline curbs stalling on accommodation. *(clause a, PDF p.143)*
- **Accommodation now the first option** — New `i) offer the employee an accommodation` becomes the Employer's first listed duty (ahead of transitional work / paying previous rate). Stronger, accommodation-first duty. *(clause c.i, PDF p.143)*
- **"Previous rate of pay" now defined** — New clause d) defines it as pre-disability straight-time rate in the pre-disability position, for approved hours up to pre-disability FTE, with seniority/service accruals and benefits on those hours. Removes ambiguity that could shortchange members. *(clause d, PDF p.144)*
- **4-week deadline retained** — Within four weeks of accepting the medical and a fit-to-return confirmation, the Employer must accommodate, offer transitional work, or pay previous rate. *(clause c, PDF p.143)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Pay offset against LTD/WorkSafeBC** — New clause d) reduces the "previous rate of pay" by any LTD insurer or WorkSafeBC voc-rehab amounts received. Prevents double-recovery, but members on those benefits get a net top-up only, not the full previous rate. *(clause d, PDF p.144)*
