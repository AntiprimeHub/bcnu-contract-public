# MOA – SANE/FNE Witness Remuneration — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-sane-fne-witness-remuneration.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument; new Appendix)

**Tally:** ✅ 2 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **Paid court-witness time for SANE/FNEs** — closes a gap: casual SANE/FNEs, and regular SANE/FNEs subpoenaed on an unscheduled day, were ineligible for paid Art. 34 court-appearance leave. Now the Employer pays their applicable CA rate for time testifying **and** reasonable prep time when subpoenaed by Crown Counsel to testify to work-arising facts. *(clauses A–F, 1a–b)*
- **Mileage + associated costs covered** — Employer provides mileage allowance per Art. 57.02(C) and other associated costs under policy, where not already reimbursed elsewhere. *(clause 3)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Must remit outside fees; single-employer claim** — any other reimbursement/fees/compensation the SANE/FNE receives for the court attendance must be remitted to the Employer, and reimbursement may be sought from only one HEABC employer per instance. No double-dipping, and the benefit is offset by external payments rather than additive. *(clauses 2, 4)*
