# Article 47 – Leave – Indigenous Child Care — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-47-leave-indigenous-child-care.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-47-workers-compensation.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gain · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **New Indigenous Child Care Leave** — Article 47 is retitled and given an entirely new body. Effective **April 1, 2026**, an employee with an established relationship/cultural-or-traditional responsibility toward an Indigenous child, who is authorized to provide daily care in place of the parent(s) by **(A) an Indigenous governing entity or (B) MCFD (kinship care provider)**, may take **up to 62 consecutive weeks of unpaid leave**; leave **will be granted upon request**. Treated as continuous employment with **maternity/parental-equivalent benefits and accruals** (excl. SEB). 14 days' return notice; returns to former position. *(Art 47, PDF p.102/103)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Old WCB clause struck but NOT lost** — The deleted old 47(A) ("All employees shall be covered by the provisions of the Workers' Compensation Act") was only a coverage pointer; the substantive WCB **leave-with-net-pay** entitlement lives in **Article 42.07 (Leave – Workers' Compensation)**, which is preserved and in fact strengthened in this round (reopened-claim eligibility, replenished sick credits). No WCB entitlement is lost in the retitling. *(former 47(A), PDF p.102; cf. 42.07, PDF p.94-95)*
