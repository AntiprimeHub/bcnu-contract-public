# Article 32 – Occupational Health and Safety Program — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-32-occupational-health-and-safety-program.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-32-occupational-health-and-safety-program.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 9 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 3 neutral · ⚠️ 4 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **32.03(D), new** — New enforceable entitlement: where employees may be exposed to biological agents, chemicals, or substances (explicitly including **illicit/street drugs and unknown-composition substances**), the Employer must complete a risk assessment and, in consultation with the JOHSC, implement controls — may include a site-specific exposure control plan, training, and PPE. *(32.03(D), PDF p.79)*
- **32.03(E), new** — Biological exposure control plans must be built on the **precautionary principle** as defined by WorkSafeBC and informed by public-health guidance/orders. *(32.03(E), PDF p.79)*
- **32.03(F), new** — Express protection: **no employee may be disciplined for a refusal to work** when excused under the Workers Compensation Act / OHS Regulation. *(32.03(F), PDF p.79)*
- **32.03(C)** — OHS supervisory training is expanded to **annual refresher training** for employees performing supervisor duties, and must now cover the WCA, OHS Regulation, and supervisor roles/responsibilities. *(32.03(C), PDF p.79)*
- **32.05, new sub-clause** — New entitlement after a work-related traumatic incident: Employer must consult the employee and **can release them for the rest of the shift without loss of pay** and **provide transportation to medical care or home** if they can't travel safely. *(32.05, PDF p.81)*
- **32.06** — Psychological factors language tightened to "thirteen (13) **psychological** factors"; Employer's CSA Standard implementation must now **track future amendments** to the Standard. *(32.06, PDF p.82)*
- **32.06, new** — Health Authorities/PHC must **make available information and education** on workplace factors affecting psychological health and safety (hazard reduction + factors that promote it). *(32.06, PDF p.83)*
- **32.07, new clause (Sexual and Domestic Violence)** — Brand-new protection: Employer must revise/develop a sexual & domestic violence policy (reporting process, individualized safety plans, confidentiality, no adverse action for good-faith reports); must run a risk assessment + employee-centred safety plan when aware of risk; trauma-informed supports. *(32.07(a)-(c), PDF p.84)* **Note: policy due within 180 days of ratification — deferred.**
- **32.08, new clause (Violence Risk Mitigation)** — Consolidates and **strengthens** the prior violence provisions (relocated from 32.03(B)-(D),(F)): adds duty to communicate risks/measures to employees, broader hazard info-sharing covering **weapon use and threatening statements** plus pre-admission/transfer/care-transition notification, and a mandatory **post-incident investigation with corrective actions communicated to affected employees**. *(32.08(A)-(F), PDF p.83-84)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Relettering & wording cleanup** — 32.03 clauses relettered (old E→B, G→C, H→D, J→E, K→F) after old (B)(C)(D)(F) moved to new 32.08; "healthful"→"healthy", "behavioral"→"behavioural", "&"→"and", PHC abbreviation, "j"→"J" capitalization. No substantive change. *(32.01/32.03/32.06, PDF p.75-83)*
- **JOHSC minutes mechanism modernized** — Replaces the old "request SWITCH BC develop a universal minutes format within 60 days" with onboarding to the **OHS Resource Centre**; minutes flow through the Centre once onboarded, monthly Union-copy obligation only applies pre-onboarding. Net process change, roughly neutral. *(32.01, PDF p.77)*
- **32.01 JOHSC rules of procedure** — Adds restatement that each JOHSC must establish its own rules of procedure per the WCA/OHS Regulation (codifies existing legal requirement). *(32.01, PDF p.75)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **32.07 sexual/domestic violence policy is a future deliverable** — the protection is a policy the Employer must produce **within 180 days of ratification**; the article sets requirements but the actual safety plans depend on that policy being written and applied. *(32.07(a), PDF p.84)*
- **Many duties are "consult JOHSC / raise concern" routes, not hard entitlements** — e.g., 32.03(D) controls are decided "in consultation with the JOHSC," and an employee's recourse if unhappy is to "raise their concern" via supervisor/JOHSC/reporting process — process, not a guaranteed outcome. *(32.03(D), PDF p.79)*
- **JOHSC info-disclosure depends on OHS Resource Centre onboarding** — the every-6-months data dump to the Union (Excel/.csv) only runs "until" JOHSCs onboard to the Resource Centre; after onboarding the Union relies on Centre access. Verify the Union retains equivalent visibility post-onboarding. *(32.01, PDF p.76-77)*
- **32.08 violence training is relocated, not net-new** — old 32.03(F) violence-prevention training language is moved verbatim into 32.08(F); confirm nothing was dropped in the move (it appears intact). *(32.08(F), PDF p.83-84)*
