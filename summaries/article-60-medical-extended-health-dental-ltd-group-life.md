## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **60.01 Medical (MSP) unchanged** — employer still pays 100% of MSP premium; condition of employment; effective first of month after hire. *(60.01, PDF p.192)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **EHC, Dental, LTD, Group Life, AD&D all moved from Article 60 into the EFNBT** — each section's substantive language (incl. the explicit "Employer shall pay 100% of premium" guarantees for EHC, dental, group life; the $50,000 life amount; $350 vision; $1,000 hearing aid; $2,750 ortho; 60% dental "B"/"C"; LTD until age 65) is struck and replaced by "the EFNBT provides … in accordance with the Plan Document." GAIN: consolidated under union/joint trust control with upgraded benefits (per item 6). WATCH-OUT: the standalone collective-agreement 100%-employer-premium guarantees are removed from Article 60; coverage terms now live in the trust's Plan Document, which Trustees can amend. *(60.02-60.06, PDF pp.192-194)*
- **60.04 Dependents definition deleted** — the eligible-dependent definition (ref. Articles 46.01-46.03) is struck with no replacement shown in this schedule; dependent eligibility presumably moves to the Plan Document. Confirm it is carried forward, not lost. *(60.04, PDF p.193)*

## Deferred (post-ratification)
- All Article 60 amendments **effective Apr 1 2027**, contingent on a mutually agreeable Trust Agreement. *(Schedule 2 header, PDF p.192)*
