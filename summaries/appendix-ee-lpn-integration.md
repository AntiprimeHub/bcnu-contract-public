# Appendix EE — LPN Integration — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/appendix-ee-lpn-integration.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/appendix-ee-*.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 0 gains · ❌ 2 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ❌ Losses
- **§2 non-applications dropped** — The old list of CA provisions that "do not apply to LPNs" (Art 11.04(A)(9) client-specific casuals, 11.04(J)(2) casual probation, 26.02(1) split shifts, 28.06 OR/PAR/ER/ICU/CCU premium, Appendix Q) is struck with NO relocation. Removing these carve-outs could newly subject LPNs to those provisions. *(old EE §2 a–e, PDF p. — struck, not re-inserted)*
- **§3.a 15-hr grandfather dropped** — The transition rule letting pre-May-11-2016 LPNs who worked <15 hrs/wk keep Regular Part-Time status (until they or the Employer change the schedule) is struck with NO relocation. Affected LPNs lose an express RPT-status protection. *(old EE §3.a / Art 11.03(a), struck, not re-inserted)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Verbatim relocations (no entitlement change)** — Most of Appendix EE moved word-for-word into the body articles: Superior Benefits incl. Eagle Ridge MOU → **6.06**; dual-registrant "pay the rate of the job" wage rule → **18.0X**; ≤**1.0 FTE** / one-status rule → **11.01(B)**; and all of §4 Seniority (April 15 2013 date, 1950-hrs/yr casual cap, two-separate-lists rule, frozen/reactivated lists, Art 13.06 sortable list, Art 51 portability) → **13.0X (A)–(H)**. The big strike-through is administrative — these entitlements are preserved unchanged. *(old EE §1, §4.j, §4 → Arts 6.06, 11.01(B), 13.0X, 18.0X; PDF pp.1–6)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Classification Transition / Red-Circled (old §3.b) gutted in the move** — Relocated to Art 62 as only **2 sentences** ("certain LPNs were entitled to wage protection and received monetary increases… Effective **April 1, 2019** wage rate 'Red-Circled'"). The Art 62 paraphrase DROPS the operative protections: the interim **'Green-Circled'** status, the **classified Level 1 / paid at Level 2** mechanic, the **special-premium** alternative, "receive all monetary increases… **unless they voluntarily transfer, resign, or retire**" (forfeiture condition), the **March 31, 2019** protection-end date, and "**wage increment progression will continue**." Dressed as a housekeeping relocation, but enforceable detail protecting red-circled LPNs was lost. *(old EE §3.b vs Art 62 insert, PDF p. ~3 / Art 62)*
