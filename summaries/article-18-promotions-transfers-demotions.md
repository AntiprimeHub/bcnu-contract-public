# Article 18 – Promotions, Transfers & Demotions — Gains & Losses

Note: the 18.01 First Consideration text (incl. (B) Non-Specialty expedited filling) is shown as **context only** — no `<ins>`/`<del>` marks, so it is unchanged here. Only 18.02 and 18.06 have actual edits.

## ✅ Gains
- **LPN→RN/RPN $200 raise floor** — 18.06 Salary on Promotion now expressly applies to nurses moving from an **LPN position to an RN or RPN position**, guaranteeing them the standard minimum $200/month promotion increase. Effective **the first pay period after April 1, 2026**. *(18.06, PDF p.65)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **18.02 — 30-day placement can be waived** — The Employer's duty to place a successful applicant within thirty (30) days of the posted start date is now subject to **"unless mutually agreed to by the Employer and Union."** Adds flexibility but also a route to extend the 30-day placement window. *(18.02, PDF p.64)*

## Deferred
- LPN→RN/RPN promotion-pay treatment takes effect **first pay period after April 1, 2026**. *(18.06, PDF p.65)*
