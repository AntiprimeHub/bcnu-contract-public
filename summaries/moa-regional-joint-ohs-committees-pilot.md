# MOA – Regional Joint OHS Committees Pilot — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-regional-joint-ohs-committees-pilot.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument)

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 2 neutral · ⚠️ 3 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **New 2-year pilot** — Establishes one **Regional OHS Committee** per Health Authority/PHC consolidating OHS, Psychological Health & Safety, and Violence Prevention into a single coordinated body — a modest structural gain for consistency/coordination. Commences within **120 days of ratification**. *(MOA ¶2-3, PDF p.172)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Advisory body only** — Committee gives "guidance and recommendations" on policies, training, risk assessments, WorkSafeBC orders, and corrective actions; it is **not established under the Workers Compensation Act** and creates no individual entitlement. *(MOA ¶4 & ¶8, PDF p.172)*
- **Sample letter appended** — Non-binding NBA/HEABC support letter expecting terms-of-reference within 2 months of first meeting and ≥1 OHS project annually; sets expectations, not obligations. *(sample letter, PDF p.174)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Time-limited pilot (2 years)** — At the end the committee self-assesses and decides whether to continue; no guarantee it survives. *(MOA ¶9, PDF p.172)*
- **Absorbs the Appendix G regional VP sub-committee** — for the pilot's duration the Regional OHS Committee **replaces and assumes** the obligations of the regional Violence Prevention sub-committee under Appendix G (Addressing Workplace Violence). If the pilot lapses, confirm the VP sub-committee role reverts cleanly. *(MOA ¶6, PDF p.172)*
- **Explicitly NOT an escalation pathway** — the MOA states it "is not an escalation pathway for local JOHSC concerns," so local committee issues do not gain a new route up through this body. *(MOA ¶6, PDF p.172)*
