# MOA – Health and Welfare Benefits – Key Terms — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-health-and-welfare-benefits-key-terms.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument; plain-language summary of the EFNBT deal)

NEW one-page "key terms" summary of the EFNBT. Benefit-enhancement items duplicate item 6 of the main MOA (not re-tallied here); this file adds the **funding/cost-allocation** terms. PDF p.181 (doc p.179).

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 2 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **NBA exclusive amendment authority over EHC, dental, life, AD&D from Apr 1 2029** — union control over benefit design. *(key term 1, PDF p.181)*
- **Employers pay full actual cost of EHC/Dental/Life/AD&D for first 2 years** — employer covers actual cost through EFNBT contributions Apr 2027–Mar 2029. *(key term 4, PDF p.181)*
- **Employer absorbs LTD experience risk** — employer increases contributions when LTD costs exceed projections by up to 2%, with further top-ups above 4%. *(key term 6, PDF p.181)*
- **$175 million NBA seed funding** — one-time NBA contribution for long-term EFNBT sustainability. *(key term 7, PDF p.181)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Benefit-enhancement list (items 2a–2q)** — identical to main-MOA item 6 (Group Life/AD&D to $250k, deductible removal, paramedical/vision/dental/ortho increases, LTD indexing, NP language). Tallied under `moa-health-and-welfare-benefits.md`. *(key term 2, PDF p.181)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **From Apr 1 2029, Employers only cover admin costs above 0.2% of payroll** — admin expenses within 0.2% are borne by the trust (i.e. members' funding), not the employer. *(key term 5, PDF p.181)*
- **EFNBT funded by employer % -of-payroll contributions, not a cost guarantee** — "designed with actuarial analysis for long-term sustainability"; sustainability now depends on the formula and trust management rather than an open-ended employer promise. *(key terms 3,5,6, PDF p.181)*

## Deferred (post-ratification)
- EFNBT commencement + all enhancements: **Apr 1 2027**. *(key term 2)*
- NBA exclusive design authority; employer admin-cost contribution above 0.2% payroll: **Apr 1 2029**. *(key terms 1,5)*
