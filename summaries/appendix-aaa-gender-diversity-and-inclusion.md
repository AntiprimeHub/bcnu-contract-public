# Appendix AAA – Gender Diversity and Inclusion — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/appendix-aaa-gender-diversity-and-inclusion.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/appendix-aaa-memorandum-of-agreement-gender-diversity-and-inclusion-gener.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gain · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 2 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

Note: Although this appendix appears as clean (un-marked-up) text in the booklet, it is **already in the in-force 2022–2025 agreement almost verbatim**. The baseline (`current/sections/appendix-aaa-…`) confirms the 8-week leave, support plan, personal-info correction and anti-discrimination clauses **already exist**. The **only tracked change in this settlement** is the Bathrooms/Change Rooms item B edit — so that is the only genuine 2025–2029 gain. (The other entitlements are real but pre-existing; see "Already in your contract" below.)

## ✅ Gains
- **Inclusive-signage deadline added (item B)** — the one tracked edit: signage welcoming diverse genders must now be posted **outside and inside** all bathroom/changeroom facilities **within 180 days of ratification** (the old text just said "post signage in all … facilities" — no deadline, no inside/outside requirement). *(Bathrooms/Change Rooms item B, PDF p.163)*

## ➖ Neutral / Aspirational
- **Anti-discrimination protections (deadnaming/misgendering/doxxing)** — Restates that gender identity/expression are protected under the BC Human Rights Code and names prohibited conduct; affirming but largely declaratory of existing law. *(clause 5, PDF p.162)*
- **Policy-review / commitment language** — Parties "will actively engage," Employers "will review policies" and "make resources available," and the MOA defers further work to the DEI Working Group; aspirational, no hard deliverable dates. *(clauses 1, 2, 6, 7 + closing line, PDF p.162)*

## ℹ️ Already in your contract (carried forward unchanged — NOT new in this deal)
These are real entitlements, but they were **won in a prior round** (note the "Effective April 1, 2023" date) and are merely reprinted here — do **not** count them as 2025–2029 gains:
- **8 weeks paid gender-affirming medical leave** — Effective **April 1, 2023**, a cumulative **eight (8) weeks** of leave **with pay** for gender-affirming medical procedures and revisions. *(Gender-Affirming Medical Leave, PDF p.162-163)*
- **Gender-affirming support plan on request** (clause 3) and **right to correct personal info** — legal name/gender on records, seniority lists, nametags, IDs, email, org charts, coverage and schedules (clause 4). *(clauses 3–4, PDF p.162)*
- **Single-occupant facility confirmation to NBA within 6 months** (item A) — also pre-existing. *(Bathrooms/Change Rooms item A, PDF p.163)*

DEFERRED: inclusive bathroom/changeroom signage (outside & inside) due **within 180 days of ratification** — the only new, deferred item in this appendix.
