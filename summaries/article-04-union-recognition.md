## ✅ Gains
- **New 4.03 Proposed Exclusions** — When the Employer converts an included (union) position to an excluded (non-union) one, it must now **notify the Union in writing**, with the org chart, job description, and reasons. The Union can demand a meeting (within **30 days**) to review the exclusion. Adds transparency and a check on bargaining-unit erosion; purely additive — no existing right is removed. *(Art. 4.03(1)-(2), PDF p.42 / doc p.40)*
