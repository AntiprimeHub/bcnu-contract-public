# Article 43 – Leave – Special and Personal — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-43-leave-special-and-personal.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-43-leave-special-and-personal.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 5 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 2 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **43.01(B)(8) — new citizenship-ceremony leave** — Effective **April 1, 2027**, up to **1 day (7.5h)** of special leave to attend the employee's own Canadian citizenship ceremony, max **1 day per lifetime**. New entitlement. *(43.01(B)(8), PDF p.96/97)*
- **43.01(B)(5) — compassionate add-on tracks the 6-day increase** — The "1 day may be added to compassionate leave" item now references **six (6)** days (was five), consistent with the Art 33 increase. *(43.01(B)(5), PDF p.96/97)*
- **43.02 — Personal Leave rewritten with concrete, expanded rights** — The thin 2-day stub is replaced by a full structure that **locks in the April 1, 2023 Joint Interpretation** (which takes precedence over the article if they conflict) and adds enforceable mechanics below. *(43.02, PDF p.98)*
- **43.02(B) — double-time for working a scheduled personal day** — If an employee voluntarily works during a scheduled personal leave, the day is paid out at straight time **and** they earn **double their regular rate** for hours worked. New. *(43.02(B), PDF p.99)*
- **43.02 — short-notice personal leave + casual/TAC eligibility** — New right to take personal leave on **<24 hrs notice** for an urgent unanticipated matter (not subject to operational requirements); and casuals temporarily appointed to regular for ≥6 months become eligible, with unused days preserved/paid out. *(43.02, PDF p.99)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **43.01(B) days→hours restatement is FTE-neutral** — The MOA hours version mirrors the days list at **1 day = 7.5h** (marriage/parental 37.5h = 5 days; care 15h = 2 days; DSV 22.5h = 3 days; citizenship 7.5h = 1 day). Quantums match; no gain/loss. *(43.01(B), PDF p.97)*
- **43.01(B) margin renumbering** — Items (4)–(8) carry handwritten renumbering (up by one) to align the days and hours lists; substance preserved. *(43.01(B), PDF p.96)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **43.02 — eligibility now gated by leave status on Jan 1 (43.02(C))** — New rule: employees **on a leave of absence as of Jan 1 get no personal days that year**; those returning in the latter 6 months get only **1** (max 2). The old stub had no such gate. Likely codifies existing Joint-Interpretation practice, but for an employee on leave over Jan 1 this is a concrete eligibility restriction — confirm against the 2023 Joint Interpretation. *(43.02(C), PDF p.98)*
