## ✅ Gains
- **44.01(I) — leave for affiliated-union leadership roles** — New clause: an employee **elected/appointed as President (or equivalent full-time leadership role)** of a provincial, national or international union/federation/labour organization affiliated with the NBA is granted leave of absence for the **full duration of their term**. The following paragraph protects seniority, increment date, and vacation/sick/special-leave accumulation, and the Employer continues benefit premiums (Union reimburses). *(44.01(I), PDF p.100)*
