# MOA – HEABC Membership for Unionized LTC/AL Operators — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-heabc-membership-ltc-al-operators.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument; MOH/HEABC/NBA)

**Tally:** ✅ 2 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 3 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **Extends NBA contract coverage to more nurses** — reverses the 2009 policy that let publicly funded LTC/AL operators avoid HEABC membership and bargain cheaper deals. Operators meeting criteria transition into HEABC, and HEABC/NBA will negotiate a template Transition Agreement to move Facilities-bargaining-unit employees onto the **NBA Collective Agreement**. This is coverage expansion, not just admin. *(clauses 4, 6)*
- **Anti-subcontracting condition** — continued wage-levelling funding is made contingent on **no subcontracting** of work currently done by unionized staff, protecting existing union jobs. *(clause 3)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Phased over 2 years** — transition runs **Oct 1, 2026 – Sept 30, 2028**: Phase One (to Sept 30, 2027) targets 50% of qualifying unionized operators, Phase Two the remainder. "Target," not guaranteed, and requires regulatory amendments to the *Health Care Employers Regulation*. *(clauses 7, 8)*
- **Eligibility gated by funding threshold** — only operators meeting "HEABC membership criteria" (Health Organization receiving majority provincial funding, services ≥ $250,000/yr) transition. Smaller or privately funded operators are excluded. *(clause 1)*
- **Wage-levelling can be cut for non-qualifying operators** — MOH commits to wage levelling only "until at least March 31, 2027" for qualifying operators; for others currently receiving it but not meeting criteria, MOH may end it on 90 days' notice. Government already announced it will stop levelling for fully privately funded operators — those nurses may lose parity. *(WHEREAS clauses + clause 2)*
