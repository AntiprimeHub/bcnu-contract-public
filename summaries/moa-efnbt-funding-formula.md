# EFNBT Funding Formula — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-efnbt-funding-formula.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument)

NEW standalone agreement (no tracked changes; all plain text) setting the numeric formula by which Employers fund the EFNBT. Executed May 21 2026. PDF pp.195-197 (doc pp.193-195).

**Tally:** ✅ 3 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 2 neutral · ⚠️ 4 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **$175M NBA seed contribution, locked until after Mar 31 2029** — one-time Apr 1 2027 contribution for long-term sustainability; Trustees cannot draw on it to fund benefits before then, protecting the cushion. *(¶15, PDF p.196)*
- **Employer contribution rises 14.00% → 14.25% of payroll from Apr 1 2031** — defined, escalating employer funding. *(¶¶18-19, PDF pp.196-197)*
- **Employers retain liability for pre-Apr-1-2027 claims** — disability income/benefits for members disabled before Apr 1 2027, and EHC/dental/life/AD&D claims incurred before that date, stay an employer obligation at March 30 2025 plan levels until closed. *(¶¶10-12, PDF pp.195-196)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Defined contribution rates** — Apr 2027–Mar 2029: 3.90% of payroll + actual EHC/Dental/Life/AD&D utilization, reconciled to actual annually; Apr 2029–Mar 2031: 14.00%; thereafter 14.25%. *(¶¶17-19, PDF pp.196-197)*
- **Allocation of pre-/post-transition responsibility + optional HBT asset/liability transfer** — EFNBT covers claims incurred after Mar 31 2027; HEABC/NBA may negotiate a transfer of HBT assets/liabilities. *(¶¶13-14, PDF p.196)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Funding is a fixed %-of-payroll formula, not a cost guarantee** — after the 2-year reconciled startup, employer obligation is a flat 14.00%/14.25% of payroll; if real benefit costs outrun that, the gap falls on the trust (member-funded), subject only to the LTD risk-share. GAIN: predictable, defined funding + union control. WATCH-OUT: benefit security now depends on the formula holding. *(¶¶17-20, PDF pp.196-197)*
- **LTD risk-sharing is capped** — employer tops up only above a 3.90%-of-payroll baseline: ≤2% over → small uplift; 2–4% over → fixed 0.078% of payroll; >4% over → 0.078% plus 50% of the excess × 3.55%. Employers do NOT cover 100% of LTD overruns; residual LTD cost risk sits with the trust. *(¶¶21-23, PDF pp.196-197)*
- **Admin expenses above 0.2% of payroll shift to additional employer contribution only from Apr 1 2029** — within-0.2% admin is trust-funded. *(¶20, PDF p.197)*
- **"Payroll" base excludes premiums/allowances/differentials** — contributions computed on straight-time hours of regular employees only, narrowing the funding base. *(¶9, PDF p.195)*

## Deferred (post-ratification)
- Funding begins **Apr 1 2027**; $175M NBA contribution **Apr 1 2027** (usable after **Mar 31 2029**); rate step-ups **Apr 1 2029** and **Apr 1 2031**. *(¶¶15-19)*
