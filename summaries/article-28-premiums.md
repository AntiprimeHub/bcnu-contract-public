# Article 28 – Premiums — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-28-premiums.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-28-premiums.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **28.01(A) Evening premium** — $1.40 → **$1.90** (1st pay period after Apr 1, 2026), then **$2.15** (Apr 1, 2027), then **$2.32** (Apr 1, 2028). +66% over the term; all increases deferred to/after 2026. *(28.01(A), PDF p.72)*
- **28.05 Regular premium** — $2.15 → **$3.00** (1st pp after Apr 1, 2026), then **$3.20** (Apr 1, 2027), then **$3.60** (Apr 1, 2028). +67% over the term; all deferred. *(28.05, PDF p.73)*
- **28.06 Specialty premium — eligibility expanded** — $2.00/hr now also covers **NICU/PICU** (added to ICU/CCU line). Same dollar value, broader coverage. *(28.06(iii), PDF p.74)*
- **28.06 Level 1 OR & ER ortho-tech eligibility** — Effective 1st pp following Apr 1, 2026, **Level 1 nurses permanently assigned to OR**, and **Level 1 nurses assigned to ER as orthopaedic technologists**, become eligible for the $2.00 premium. Deferred. *(28.06, PDF p.74)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **28.01(B) Night premium** — Unchanged at **$5.00/hr**; the strike-through only removes the superseded Apr 2022 ($4.25) line. No new money. (28.02 Weekend, 28.03, 28.04 also show no change vs baseline.) *(28.01(B), PDF p.72)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **28.06 "Level 3/4/5 nurses permanently assigned"** — Old text covered "regular employees... employed in [dept] with permanently assigned staff." New text reframes eligibility as **Level 3/4/5 nurses permanently assigned to** the department. This restructure could narrow who counts as eligible (e.g., excludes employees below Level 3 in those units, except the explicitly re-added Level 1 OR/ER cases). Dollar value unchanged at $2.00. ⚠️ verify against final language. *(28.06, PDF p.74)*
