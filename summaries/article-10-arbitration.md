# ARTICLE 10 – Arbitration — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-10-arbitration.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-10-arbitration.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **10.01(F) Rapid Dispute Resolution (trial)** — New **optional, by-mutual-agreement** Rapid Dispute Resolution Process (Appendix FF.1) offering faster, lower-cost handling of disputes. Title also updated to "...and Dispute Resolution Methods." Potential for quicker outcomes without giving up regular arbitration. *(Art. 10.01(F), PDF p.51 / doc p.49)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **RDR is non-precedent-setting and trial-only** — Unlike the standard streams (which are "precedent setting, final and binding"), the new process is expressly **non-precedent-setting** and runs only "for the life of the 2025–2029 Agreement" on a trial basis. Use it and you forfeit a binding precedent. It is opt-in/mutual-agreement only, so it is not a forced hurdle — but check Appendix FF.1 for what rights are traded for speed before agreeing to use it. *(Art. 10.01(F), PDF p.51 / doc p.49)*
