# Appendix RR – Employed Student Nurse (ESN) — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/appendix-rr-employed-student-nurse.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/appendix-rr-memorandum-of-agreement-employed-student-nurse-esn.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 2 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **ESN hourly rate jumps to $33.09 → $34.08 → $35.10 → $36.15.** New 4-year wage ladder for undergraduate student nurses, effective first pay period after **April 1 2025 ($33.09)**, **April 1 2026 ($34.08)**, **April 1 2027 ($35.10)**, **April 1 2028 ($36.15)**. The 2025 rate of $33.09 is up from the old top rate of $32.13 (~3%/yr; **+12.5%** above the prior $32.13 top rate by 2028). *(Wage Entitlement (F) i–iv, PDF p.1 of MOA)*
- **A 4th contract year added.** The old MOA capped out at April 1 2024; the new one adds an **April 1 2028** rate, extending guaranteed ESN wage coverage by a full year. *(clause iv, PDF p.1 of MOA)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **First two raises retroactive, last two deferred.** With the deal signed May 2026, the 2025 and 2026 ESN rates are effectively retroactive on ratification, while the **April 2027 ($35.10) and April 2028 ($36.15)** rates are future/deferred. *(clauses iii–iv, PDF p.1 of MOA)*
