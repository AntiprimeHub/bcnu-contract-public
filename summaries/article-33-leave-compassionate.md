# Article 33 – Leave – Compassionate — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-33-leave-compassionate.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-33-leave-compassionate.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 3 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **33.02 — extra day of paid compassionate leave** — Paid compassionate leave rises from **5 → 6 work days** (37.5h → 45h), effective **60 days after ratification of the 2025-2029 agreement**. The 6 days need not be consecutive. *(33.02, PDF p.87/88)*
- **33.01 — broader eligible-relatives list** — Compassionate leave now covers **current or former foster child or ward** and **child-in-law**, on top of the existing family list. *(33.01, PDF p.87)*
- **33.01 — leave allowed before death ("imminent")** — Employees may now take compassionate leave **before** a family member dies once death is imminent — defined as admitted to hospice, approved for MAID, or a physician's prognosis of death within 3 months. *(33.01, PDF p.87)*
