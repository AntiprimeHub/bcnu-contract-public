# Article 11 – Definition of Employee Status & Benefit Entitlement — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-11-definition-of-employee-status-and-benefit-entitlement.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-11-definition-of-employee-status-and-benefit-entitlement.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 2 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **$1,000–$2,430/yr** — New **Casual Hours Incentive (11.04(L))**, effective **Jan 1, 2027**: casuals working ≥600 cumulative straight-time hours for one Employer in a calendar year get a $1,000 annual payment, rising $110 per extra 100 hours, to a max $2,430 at ≥1,900 hours. Only straight-time hours in casual status count. *(11.04(L), PDF p.54)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **11.04(E)(8)** — Definition of "block of work" tightened: any shift/block/combination of shifts that has **on-call attached must be offered and accepted with the on-call** (replaces bare "any combination of shifts"). Aligns call-in mechanics with the new on-call shift category; neutral process clarification. *(11.04(E)(8), PDF p.52)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **11.04(A)(10)** — New casual category: **"Shifts with on-call attached."** Expands the kinds of work casuals can be called in for; pairs with 11.04(E)(8) requiring the on-call be accepted together with the shift. Could mean more guaranteed on-call obligation bundled into casual call-ins. *(11.04(A)(10), PDF p.52)*
- **Incentive is conditional/reviewable** — Must still be employed by that Employer as of Dec 31 to collect; and the parties may **reallocate the funding in future bargaining** if it doesn't drive more casual hours. Not a permanent guaranteed benefit. *(11.04(L), PDF p.54)*
