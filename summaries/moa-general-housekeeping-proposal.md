# General Housekeeping Proposal — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-general-housekeeping-proposal.md` · Baseline: multiple (per item)

> The MOA's own preamble calls these "housekeeping … not intended to substantively alter" rights. That is mostly true, but NOT entirely: it deletes six MOAs/appendices outright, and at least two of those carried live (if niche) entitlements. Effective on ratification of the 2025–2029 agreement.

**Tally:** ✅ 0 gains · ❌ 2 losses · ➖ 11 neutral · ⚠️ 3 watch-outs

## ❌ Losses
- **Appendix Q deleted — path to REGULAR status for home-support client-specific nurses removed.** App Q let a nurse working 15–37.5 hrs/wk in client-specific assignments for >4 months convert to regular status (with displacement/replacement protections). Pure deletion, no relocation. The parties had flagged it dormant/under-review since 1998, but it is still in the in-force agreement, so for any nurse relying on it this is a real lost conversion route. Companion deletions strip the matching casual category (11.04(A)(9)), its 487.5-hr probation (11.04(J)(2)), and the split-shift carve-out (26.02(1)). *(App Q + Art 11.04, 26.02; PDF p.155–161 / 3–4 of 28)*
- **Appendix PP deleted — Primary & Community Care transition protections gone.** Removes the commitment that nurses transitioning to primary/community care get an individual learning-needs assessment with any education gap filled *before* being required to do the work, plus the change-management/Labour Adjustment Plan and clinical-resource-nurse funding. Framed as implementation/transition language, but the "education before required work" safeguard was a concrete protection. *(App PP; PDF p.463–525 / 12–13 of 28)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Art 37.04 Domestic & Sexual Violence Leave relocated VERBATIM to new Art 41** (Art 41 retitled Public Office → Domestic and Sexual Violence; the old Public Office text moves up to Art 36.02). Entitlement unchanged: 5 paid + 5 unpaid days + up to 15 weeks additional unpaid, benefit continuity, casual carve-out, legislation-prevails clause all preserved. *(Art 37.04 → 41; PDF p.251–297 / 6–7 of 28)*
- **Appendix P Incentive Payment (Pre/Post-Retirees) relocated VERBATIM to Art 49.0X.** Annual payment = what Employer would have contributed to MPP/PSPP, payable after Dec 31, to RRSP or direct — word-for-word the same. *(App P → Art 49.0X; PDF p.543–599 / 14–15 of 28)*
- **Appendix N (Community-Based flexible-work / Munroe arbitration principle) deleted — no loss.** The Northern Interior Health Unit / Munroe (1996) interpretation principle for OT and shift premiums on flexible schedules was **already stated in the body of Art 25.07(H)**; the MOA just deletes the now-redundant Appendix N and the "(See Appendix N)" cross-ref. Substance preserved. *(App N / Art 25.07(H); PDF p.527–541 / 14 of 28)*
- **Art 46.02 Indigenous ceremonial/cultural/spiritual leave restated "five (5) days" → "thirty-seven point five (37.5) hours."** FTE-neutral for standard 7.5-hr/day staff (5 × 7.5 = 37.5). NOT a cut — see watch-out for compressed-week caveat. *(Art 46.02(a); PDF p.431–435 / 10–11 of 28)*
- **Art 38 "Maternity" → "Pregnancy" gender-neutral reword throughout** (incl. heading, 38.01–38.06, plus 11.04(A)(8) and 13.03(b) cross-refs). MOA expressly states no change to benefits/entitlements; all weeks (17 pregnancy / 61 + 62 parental / 87% SEB allowance) unchanged. *(Art 38, 11.04, 13.03; PDF p.846–982 / 24–27 of 28)*
- **Cross-reference renumbering corrected (no substance change):** 11.03(B) Art 46→60; 21.01 & 22.01 Art 64→62; 60.04 "46.01/46.02/46.03"→"60.01/60.02/60.03"; App A 14.2 Art 46→60; 25.05(A) "25.05(E)"→"25.06(E)". Pointers only — none alter an entitlement. *(One real but non-entitlement change rides along here: **61(B) adds "and 3" to the Labour Relations Code s.50 exclusion** — i.e. it broadens which statutory subsections are carved out of the agreement. Operative, but it changes no nurse's entitlement.)* *(various; PDF p.305–445 / 8–11 of 28)*
- **Art 53.03 RPN differential — statutory reference modernized.** "Nurses (Registered) Act / Nurses (Registered Psychiatric) Act" → "RN and RPN under the BC Health Professions and Occupations Act." Still $50/month; eligibility (dual RN+RPN registration) unchanged. *(Art 53.03; PDF p.379–383 / 9–10 of 28)*
- **College/statute name updates** throughout App RR and JJ.3: "Health Professions Act"→"Health Professions and Occupations Act"; "BCCNP"→"BCCNM." Naming only. *(App RR, JJ.3; PDF p.601–645 / 16 of 28)*
- **Art 32 / App D OH&S language cleanup** (large but cosmetic): "Joint Occupational Health and Safety Committee" spelled out once then abbreviated "JOHSC"; "healthful"→"healthy", "behavioral"→"behavioural", "co-operation"→"cooperation", "&"→"and", PHC/CSA abbreviations defined. No duties, funding ($1,290,000/annum unchanged), or obligations altered. *(Art 32, App D; PDF p.649–840 / 17–23 of 28)*
- **Appendix HH (Job Descriptions) deleted — SPENT.** Its only operative deadline (reduce to ≤60 job descriptions per Health Authority by March 31, 2014) is more than a decade past; a fully obsolete one-time consolidation commitment. *(App HH; PDF p.63–75 / 1–2 of 28)*
- **Appendix JJ.4 (Rural & Remote Nursing) deleted — SPENT.** A one-time joint review tied to a prior PCA's ratification timeline with a one-time $2,000,000 allocation; the review window has long closed. *(App JJ.4; PDF p.77–109 / 2 of 28)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Appendix JJ.2 (Specialty Education) deleted — funding floor disappears with no replacement shown here.** Old MOA guaranteed funding for ≥850 specialty-education FTEs (FY2016–17 + balance of that agreement). Largely time-spent, BUT the deletion removes any standing specialty-ed funding commitment from the agreement. If specialty-education funding is not secured elsewhere in the 2025–2029 settlement, this is a quiet loss — confirm against the funding/education MOAs. *(App JJ.2; PDF p.459–461 / 11 of 28)*
- **Art 46.02 — compressed-workweek Indigenous staff should confirm equivalence.** "37.5 hours" exactly equals 5 days only at 7.5 hr/day. Staff on longer compressed shifts should verify they are not effectively limited to fewer than 5 calendar days of ceremonial/cultural/spiritual leave. *(Art 46.02(a); PDF p.435 / 11 of 28)*
- **Appendix Q deletion (see Losses) — niche but real.** Any current home-support client-specific casual relying on the 15–37.5 hr regular-status conversion loses that route; affected members should confirm whether they already hold regular status or have another path. *(App Q; PDF p.155–209 / 4–5 of 28)*
