# Article 62 – Wage Schedule Classifications — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-62-wage-schedule-classifications.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-62-wage-schedule-classifications.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 0 gains · ❌ 1 loss · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 2 watch-outs

> Note: two of the items below (Red-Circled paraphrase, Eagle Ridge) physically appear in a sibling changes file, `changes/appendix-ee-lpn-integration.md` (PDF p.148), but they substantively amend **Article 62 / superior benefits** and the cross-check tracks them as "Appendix EE → Article 62," so they are folded in here.

## ❌ Losses
- **LPN→RN/RPN move becomes a "promotion" (loses Art 18.06 shield).** Effective first pay period after **April 1 2026** (deferred), an LPN moving to an RN/RPN position WILL be treated as a promotion and WILL trigger Article 18.06 requirements — reversing the current rule that such a move is "not a promotion" and does "not trigger 18.06." Adds a process hurdle that today's nurses don't face when up-classifying. *(Art 62 amendment, PDF p.113)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- Classification table, job groups (CH/DC/ED/PS), Level definitions, LPN Orthopaedic Technologist / LGN footnotes, and the OR-rate grandparenting language are restated unchanged. Adds the unchanged "merging…shall not result in any other changes" proviso. No entitlement change. *(Art 62, PDF p.113–114)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **HIDDEN LOSS — Red-Circled wage protection reduced to a 2-sentence summary.** The detailed "Classification Transition" protection from deleted Appendix EE §3.b is relocated into Article 62 as just: *"Upon integration into the NBA, certain LPNs were entitled to wage protection and received monetary increases. Effective April 1 2019, those identified LPNs had their wage rate 'Red-Circled'."* The paraphrase **DROPS** every operative protection: the interim **"Green-Circled"** status, the **March 31 2019** protection-end date, the Level-1-classified-but-paid-at-Level-2 mechanics, the **transfer/resign/retire forfeiture** condition, the **"special premium"** alternative, and **"wage increment progression continues."** The settlement calls this "housekeeping…not intended to substantively alter rights," but protections that existed in enforceable detail are now a vague restatement — verify against source PDF p.145 (struck §3.b) vs p.148 (Art 62 insert) before relying on it. *(appendix-ee changes file → Art 62 insert, PDF p.148)*
- **Eagle Ridge superior-benefit carve-out relocated (verify it survives).** The Eagle Ridge Hospital LPN superior-benefits guarantee from Appendix EE §1.b is re-inserted into Article 6.06; the broader "all other FBA/CBA superior benefits extinguished" language moves with it. Per the cross-check this moved verbatim (benign), but because both halves are wholly struck/added there is no inline diff — confirm the Eagle Ridge protection is intact. *(appendix-ee changes file → Art 6.06, PDF p.148)*
