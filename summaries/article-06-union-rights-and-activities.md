# ARTICLE 6 – Union Rights and Activities — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-06-union-rights-and-activities.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-06-union-rights-and-activities.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 1 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **6.02 Agency-nurse limits (new B–E)** — Parties declare a "common interest in ending the use of private agency nursing." Employer must **offer all shifts to bargaining-unit nurses first** before using agency nurses; HEABC must give the NBA a **quarterly utilization report**; agency nurses/contractors **may not supervise or direct** NBA members. Real new protections against agency-nurse displacement. *(Art. 6.02(B)-(E), PDF p.44 / doc p.42)*
- **6.09 Steward time at orientation (15 min)** — Union steward now gets **up to 15 minutes** to address new hires at both in-person AND virtual orientations, paid (no wage/benefit deduction). *(Art. 6.09, PDF p.45 / doc p.43)*
- **6.09 Mandatory induction video** — If onboarding is online/self-paced, the Union's induction video becomes a **mandatory** component; employees must confirm they viewed it and the Employer reports this to the local steward. Steward also gets advance notice of new-hire names and session details. *(Art. 6.09, PDF p.45 / doc p.43)*
- **6.08 Distribution to each unit** — Employer must now ensure a printed copy of the finalized Agreement is **distributed to each unit**, and post the finalized printed version online. *(Art. 6.08, PDF p.46 / doc p.44)*

## ❌ Losses
- **6.08 Fewer printed copies** — Print ratio cut from **2 copies per 3 employees** down to **1 copy per 2 employees** per worksite. Fewer physical agreements available. (Partly offset by mandated per-unit distribution + online posting above.) *(Art. 6.08, PDF p.46 / doc p.44)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **6.08 retitled** "Copies" → "Publication"; "shall be made available" → "will be made available." Cosmetic. *(Art. 6.08, PDF p.46)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Appendix O (Agency Nurses MOA) deleted** — The standalone Appendix O is struck. Its substance (quarterly report + no-supervision rule) is **re-homed verbatim into new 6.02(D)-(E)**, so protections appear preserved, not lost. Confirm the new 6.02 language fully covers everything Appendix O did before relying on this. *(Art. 6.02 / Appendix O deletion, PDF p.44 / doc p.42)*
