# Article 20 – Technological Change, Automation — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-20-technological-change-automation.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-20-technological-change-automation.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 2 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **AI / modified tech now covered** — 20.01 protection against losing employment due to technological change is extended to cover **"modifications to existing technologies"** (alongside automation and new methods), expressly naming Artificial Intelligence. Broadens the no-job-loss safeguard. *(20.01, PDF p.66)*
- **Union consultation on tech-driven layoffs** — New duty: where tech change forces significant job-duty changes resulting in layoff, the **Employer will consult the Union to explore options to keep nurses in secure and meaningful employment.** *(20.02, PDF p.66–67)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Framing language** — New preamble recognizing the "potential benefits of technological change, for example AI, in enhancing healthcare delivery." Aspirational context, no operative effect. *(20.01, PDF p.66)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Consultation, not veto** — The new layoff safeguard is a duty to *consult and explore options*, not a guarantee against tech-driven layoff; existing 28-day notice and Article 19 layoff/recall rules still govern. *(20.02, PDF p.67)*
