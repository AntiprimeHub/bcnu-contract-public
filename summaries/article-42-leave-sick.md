# Article 42 – Leave – Sick — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-42-leave-sick.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-42-leave-sick.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **42.06 — cash out OT/vacation when sick bank runs out** — Employees on unpaid leave after exhausting sick credits **may elect to have banked overtime and/or accrued vacation paid out**, providing income while awaiting LTD. New option. *(42.06, PDF p.92)*
- **42.07(A) — reopened WCB claims now covered** — Where WorkSafeBC **reopens** a claim for an employee already on permanent partial disability benefits, the employee becomes **eligible for net pay**. New protection. *(42.07(A), PDF p.94)*
- **42.07(F) — sick credits replenished on WCB approval** — When sick leave is used for a work-related emergency appointment and **WorkSafeBC later approves** the claim for that period, the **sick credits are replenished**. New. *(42.07(F), PDF p.95)*
- **42.09(A) — proof-of-need burden trimmed** — Old 42.09(A) (the 8-days'-notice/can't-arrange-off-duty test for non-emergency appointments) is **deleted**; the proof-of-need requirement now applies only to the two narrower remaining clauses. *(42.09, PDF p.93)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **42.09 re-lettering** — Old (B)/(C)/(D) renumbered to (A)/(B)/(C) after the deletion; substance unchanged. *(42.09, PDF p.93)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **42.06 — "until receipt of LTD" phrase removed** — The clause dropping the old "shall be placed on unpaid leave **until they are in receipt of long-term disability benefits**" wording, replacing the LTD cross-ref (old 46.05) with the new Article 60. Likely a renumbering/clarity update (the payout election is the real change), but verify the removal of the explicit "until LTD" bridge does not weaken the guaranteed transition to LTD. *(42.06, PDF p.92)*
