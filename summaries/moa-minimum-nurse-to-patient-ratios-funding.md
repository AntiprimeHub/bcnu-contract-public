# MOA – Minimum Nurse-to-Patient Ratios Funding — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-minimum-nurse-to-patient-ratios-funding.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument; addendum to 2023 MOU)

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gain · ❌ 1 loss · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 3 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **$100M / $200M / $300M new staffing money (HEADLINE)** — MOH commits **additional** funds to health authorities to implement Minimum Nurse-to-Patient Ratios: **$100M in FY2026/27, $200M in FY2027/28, $300M in FY2028/29 (and ongoing)**. Over and above the 2023 MOU commitments. *(clause 1a–c)*

## ❌ Losses
- **NBA waives all disputes on past 2023-MOU compliance** — the NBA will not initiate any arbitration, lawsuit, or dispute regarding the Ministry's compliance with the 2023 MOU obligations up to and including the date of this agreement. Gives up leverage on prior under-delivery. *(clause 6)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **DRAFT MOA, not final** — document is marked **"DRAFT"** and "E&OE / Without P&P," between MOH and NBA. Figures could change before execution; confirm a signed version exists. *(header)*
- **Phased / deferred** — funding ramps over three+ fiscal years; the largest tranche ($300M) only lands FY2028/29, near the end of the agreement term. Front-loaded relief is limited. *(clause 1)*
- **Funding is targeted, not guaranteed-to-nurses** — money goes to health authorities to "support implementation," with minimum-spend targets ($50M/$150M/$250M/$300M) and only soft consequences: underspend is "mutually discussed within 90 days" and "reserved for mNPR initiatives," not clawed back to nurses. Also "available to," not transferred. *(clauses 1–3)*

## Deferred / phased

- FY2027/28: $200M (min spend $150M)
- FY2028/29: $300M and ongoing (min spend $250M)
- FY2029/30: min spend $300M
