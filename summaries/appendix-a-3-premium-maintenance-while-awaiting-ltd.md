# Appendix A.3 – Premium Maintenance While Awaiting LTD — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/appendix-a-3-premium-maintenance-while-awaiting-ltd.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/appendix-a-3-memorandum-of-agreement-premium-maintenance-while-awaiting-l.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gain · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **12 → 18 months** — Premium reimbursement extended: where the LTD eligibility-wait or appeal runs past 12 months and up to **18 months**, the Union reimburses **100%** of benefit premiums from the Appendix A.4 Fund for that extra period. Six more months of benefits coverage for members awaiting LTD. *(reimbursement clause, PDF p.142)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **"from the 20-day end of the benefit continuation deadline onward"** — New phrasing clarifies the reimbursement start point (after the 20-day unpaid grace period). Reads as a clarification, not a clawback, but confirm it doesn't shorten the front-end window any member previously relied on. *(reimbursement clause, PDF p.142)*
