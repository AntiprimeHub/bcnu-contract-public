**Tally:** ✅ 0 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 3 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Working Group continued, not re-established** — Group now "continues" rather than being newly established; the old "within 120 days of ratification" set-up deadline is dropped. Same PHHRCC-run body; no member-facing entitlement. *(clauses 3, 4, PDF p.160-161)*
- **Scope/language refresh** — "under-represented groups" reframed as "historically, persistently and/or systemically oppressed (HPSO) peoples/groups"; "stakeholders" → "interest holders"; adds people with lived experience as possible guests. Definitional, no concrete commitment. *(clauses 2, 5, 7, PDF p.160-161)*
- **Mandate consolidation** — Old detailed "framework and action plan" recommendation list (and clause 9) replaced by a single cooperative-implementation paragraph covering training topics (anti-racism, gender/sexual diversity, anti-harassment, disability awareness). Recommendations only; nothing binding on employers. *(clause 7 bullets + clause 9, PDF p.161)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Completion deadline now open-ended** — Old "complete work prior to March 31, 2025" replaced by "[end of new agreement term]" (an editorial placeholder in the source, not final contract text). Confirm the executed text fixes a real date; an undefined deadline weakens accountability. *(clause 6, PDF p.161)*
