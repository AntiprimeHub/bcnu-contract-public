## ✅ Gains
- **8.01 Training observer seat** — The Union may bring **one additional attendee** to observe a U/M Committee meeting for training/onboarding, with **5 days' notice** (waivable for short-notice meetings). Helps develop new Union reps. Caveats: an employee-observer's attendance is subject to operational needs, unpaid if outside work hours, and at no extra cost to the Employer; the Employer gets a matching extra rep to keep parity. Net positive for Union capacity-building. *(Art. 8.01, PDF p.47 / doc p.45)*
