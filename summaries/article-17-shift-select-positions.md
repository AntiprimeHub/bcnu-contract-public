# Article 17.0X – Shift-Select Positions (SSP) — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-17-shift-select-positions.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-17-vacancy-postings.md` (Art. 17 "Vacancy Postings"; SSP is an entirely new clause)

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 1 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 5 watch-outs

This is a wholly NEW position type ("adding the following"). An SSP is a **regular position** (fixed 0.4–1.0 FTE) that is NOT tied to a fixed master rotation; the nurse selects shifts each booking window to make up their FTE. Read both the gains and watch-outs together — it expands flexibility but also creates an employer-controlled, schedule-unstable line that can absorb baseline FTE and relief work.

## ✅ Gains
- **Nurse-chosen schedule** — SSP nurses select their own shifts each booking window rather than working a fixed rotation; intended to improve flexibility, work-life balance, and control. *(intro + D, PDF p.59–60)*
- **Seniority-ordered selection** — Shifts are assigned in **seniority order** among SSP nurses; a 3-week selection process with a reselection round if a nurse doesn't get a full allotment. *(G, PDF p.60)*
- **SSP nurses bid ahead of casuals** — During SSP shift selection, SSP nurses bid on available shifts **in priority to casual employees**. *(F, PDF p.60)*
- **Stat & holiday protections retained** — Stat holidays are inserted into the booking window (M); SSP shifts on stat holidays are paid at Art. 39.03 holiday rates (P); vacation selected via a notional master-rotation line under Art. 45.04 (N). *(M/N/P, PDF p.60)*

## ❌ Losses
- **Most of Article 25 (Scheduling) is switched off** — Except 25.08, 25.09(A) and 25.10, **Article 25 does NOT apply to SSPs**; SSP shifts are not subject to normal scheduling requirements. Standard rotation/scheduling protections that regular-line nurses enjoy are largely removed for SSP nurses. *(O, PDF p.60)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Catch-all** — Except as modified by this Article, all other Collective Agreement terms apply to SSP nurses; reassignment treated "the same as relief" and no more often than other employees. *(K, Q, PDF p.60)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Could erode regular fixed lines** — SSPs can staff **regular baseline FTE** and operate as an "enhanced float/relief pool"; employers may use vacancy/overtime/agency data to decide where to convert staffing to SSPs. Risk: fewer predictable fixed-rotation lines over time. *(intro, A, PDF p.59)*
- **Employer can assign shifts you didn't pick** — If a nurse doesn't select enough shifts to make FTE by the deadline, the **Employer may assign** shifts within the work area to fill the FTE. Schedule control is conditional. *(J, PDF p.60)*
- **Limited overtime** — SSP shifts scheduled under this Article **will not incur overtime**; OT only on hours beyond scheduled shifts. Fewer OT opportunities than regular scheduling. *(P, PDF p.60)*
- **Mandatory peak-period work** — SSP nurses MUST select ≥75 hours × FTE during peak vacation periods (Spring Break and/or Dec 15–Jan 5). Constrains time off at the most-wanted periods. *(E, PDF p.59–60)*
- **Schedule instability / supernumerary fill** — If too few shifts of the required type exist, the nurse must take whatever remains, possibly **supernumerary relief shifts**; required night/evening/weekend minimums set by the posting and changeable only by mutual agreement. *(B, I, PDF p.59–60)*
