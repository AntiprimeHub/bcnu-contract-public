# MOA – Health and Welfare Benefits — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-health-and-welfare-benefits.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-60-…`, `appendix-u/u.1/u.2-…`, `appendix-a` (LTD plan)

This MOA is a NEW instrument added wholesale. It (a) creates the **Employer-Funded Nurses' Benefits Trust (EFNBT)** to take over all health/welfare benefits effective **April 1, 2027**, (b) lists immediate plan enhancements (item 6), and (c) carries **Schedule 1** rewriting the Appendix A LTD plan. (Schedule 2 / Article 60 covered in its own summary.)

**Tally:** ✅ 16 gains · ❌ 1 loss · ➖ 2 neutral · ⚠️ 5 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **Group Life $50,000 → $250,000** — 5× increase, effective on EFNBT commencement Apr 1 2027. *(item 6a, PDF p.179)*
- **AD&D $50,000 → $250,000** — 5× increase, effective Apr 1 2027. *(item 6b, PDF p.179)*
- **$25 annual EHC deductible removed** — extended health deductible eliminated. *(item 6c, PDF p.179)*
- **Hearing aids $1,000 → $2,500 per ear / 5 yrs** — 2.5× increase. *(item 6d, PDF p.179)*
- **Vision $350 → $900 every 24 months** — and now explicitly covers eye exams. *(item 6e, PDF p.179)*
- **Massage: $4,000/member + $2,000/dependent annual limit** — replaces scheduled cuts to $1,427 (2027) / $1,145 (2028); a large improvement vs. those impending limits, but a cap where the plan previously set **no massage-specific dollar limit** (see loss). *(item 6f, PDF p.179)*
- **Acupuncture $100 → $500 per person/yr** — 5× increase. *(item 6g, PDF p.179)*
- **Chiropractic $200 → $500 per person/yr** — 2.5× increase. *(item 6h, PDF p.179)*
- **Naturopathy $200 → $500 per person/yr** — 2.5× increase [handwritten JS initial]. *(item 6i, PDF p.179)*
- **Podiatry/chiropody $300 → $500 per person/yr** — increase; "$300" handwritten-corrected to "$400" then to $500 limit; "chiropody" struck, "podiatry" retained [JS]. *(item 6j, PDF p.179)*
- **Speech therapy $100 → $1,000 per person/yr** — 10× increase. *(item 6k, PDF p.179)*
- **Mental health (psych/RSW/RCC) $900 → $1,800 per person/yr** — doubled. *(item 6l, PDF p.179)*
- **Major dental coinsurance 60% → 70%** — plan pays more of major dental. *(item 6m, PDF p.179)*
- **Orthodontia lifetime max $2,750 → $4,000** — increase. *(item 6n, PDF p.179)*
- **Shingrex (shingles) vaccine added** — new coverage. *(item 6o, PDF p.179)*
- **LTD threshold $5,843 → $8,318 (RN/RPN) / $5,110 (LPN); indexing every 2 yrs (was 4)** — 70%/50% earnings-replacement breakpoint raised sharply for RNs/RPNs as of Apr 1 2024; reviewed every two years instead of four. (LPN figure $5,110 is below the old $5,843 — see watch-out.) *(Sch 1 §5.7 + item 6p, PDF p.187/179)*

## ❌ Losses
- **Massage benefit newly capped** — massage now carries a hard annual cap ($4,000/member, $2,000/dependent) where the EHC plan previously set **no massage-specific dollar limit**; the increased-cost amendments are part-funded "from savings from a reimbursement limit on massage therapy benefits." Net better than the looming Ready-decision limits ($1,427/$1,145) but a cap where there was none before. *(item 6f + item 6 chapeau, PDF p.179)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **LTD plan language relocated to the Trust** — Schedule 1 strikes most operative LTD text from Appendix A (eligibility detail, exclusions, application, residual benefit, ERIB, successive disabilities, premiums, administration, advisory committee) because those entitlements now sit in the EFNBT Trust/Plan Document; collective-agreement-governed obligations (waiting period, sick-leave top-up, benefit continuation, appeals/CRC, rehab/RRC, GRTW, return-to-work) are preserved. Substance moves, not deleted. *(Sch 1 §§1–23, PDF pp.184-191)*
- **"medical doctor or Nurse Practitioner"** — care-during-disability requirement updated to recognize NPs (minor scope expansion / admin). *(item 6q, PDF p.179)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Benefits move into a trust funded by a formula, not a fixed employer guarantee** — equivalent to current HBT plan as of May 20 2026 plus the item-6 upgrades, but going forward governed by the Trust Agreement and Funding Formula. GAIN: dedicated funded trust + union/joint control over design. WATCH-OUT: no longer an open-ended employer promise to fund whatever the plan costs. *(items 2,5,6,9, PDF p.179)*
- **Trustees can amend the plan** — the item-6 upgrades "take effect immediately" but Trustees "will have authority to amend the benefit plan." Today's listed enhancements are not permanently locked. *(item 7, PDF p.179)*
- **NBA controls non-LTD design; LTD is joint; everything frozen until Apr 1 2029** — NBA trustees get exclusive amendment power over EHC/dental/life/AD&D; LTD amendments are joint NBA+HEABC; but before Apr 1 2029 ANY plan or administration change needs both HEABC and NBA agreement. *(item 8, PDF p.179)*
- **LPN LTD threshold ($5,110) is below the old single threshold ($5,843)** — RNs/RPNs gain ($8,318) but the LPN breakpoint is set lower than the prior uniform $5,843; for LPNs the 70%-replacement band is narrower than before. Confirm intent before characterizing as pure gain. *(Sch 1 §5.7, PDF p.187)*
- **Appendices S, T, U, U.1, U.2 terminated; deal is contingent and time-gated** — entire MOA is contingent on a mutually agreeable Trust Agreement by Jul 1 2026 (Vince Ready mediation if not); Appendix U/U.1/U.2 (incl. Ready massage limits) and Appendices S & T are terminated on signing the Trust Agreement / Apr 1 2027. If the Trust Agreement fails, the consequential amendments don't take effect. *(items 2,3,11,12, PDF p.179)*

## Deferred (post-ratification, conditional)
- All item-6 enhancements + Schedule 1 LTD changes: **effective Apr 1 2027**, contingent on a Trust Agreement (target Jul 1 2026). *(items 2,6,12, PDF p.179)*
- NBA non-LTD exclusive amendment authority + admin-cost / 0.2%-payroll mechanics: **Apr 1 2029**. *(item 8; key-terms 1,5, PDF p.179)*
