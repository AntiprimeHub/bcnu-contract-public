# Article 27 – Overtime — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-27-overtime.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-27-overtime.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gain · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **27.07 Overtime By Seniority — entitlement expanded** — Old text only applied "where a Health Authority/PHC has implemented automated call-in technology." New text **deletes that precondition**: now *any* pre-booked overtime offered >48 hours in advance must be **offered by seniority order**, regardless of whether the employer has call-in tech. This is a genuine entitlement change (more nurses get seniority-ordered OT), not just a process tweak. *(27.07, PDF p.71)*
- For employers other than Health Authorities/PHC, this takes effect **April 1, 2027** (deferred). *(27.07, PDF p.71)*
