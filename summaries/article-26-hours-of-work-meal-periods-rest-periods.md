# Article 26 – Hours of Work, Meal Periods, Rest Periods — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-26-hours-of-work-meal-periods-rest-periods.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-26-hours-of-work-meal-periods-rest-periods.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 3 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **26.03(B)(2) Missed meal period** — When a nurse on a designated-available 7.5h shift is denied the 30-min meal break, the penalty rate rises from **time-and-a-half (1.5×)** to **double time (2×)** for the meal period. Effective 1st pp after Apr 1, 2026 (deferred); the existing 1.5× penalty remains until then. *(26.03(B)(2), PDF p.69)*
- **26.03(C) Recall during meal (not designated-available)** — Same upgrade: penalty for ungranted make-up time goes from **1.5× to 2×**. Effective 1st pp after Apr 1, 2026 (deferred). *(26.03(C), PDF p.69)*
- **26.03(D) Meal-period overtime cap raised** — The maximum overtime rate for meal periods rises from **1.5× to 2×** (irrespective of Article 27 rates). Effective 1st pp after Apr 1, 2026 (deferred); this lifts the cap so the new 2× penalties above can actually be paid. *(26.03(D), PDF p.70)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **26.03(B) list renumber** — Hand-corrected printed numbering error in the (B) list (printed (1),(1),(2) → corrected to (1),(2),(3) with negotiator initials). Cross-reference "(1) above / (2) apply" preserved. Pure renumbering, no substance. *(26.03(B), PDF p.69)*
