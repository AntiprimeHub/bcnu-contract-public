# Article 37 – Leave – General — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-37-leave-general.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-37-leave-general.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 4 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **New Reservists' Leave (37.0X)** — New entitlement to **unpaid leave** for Canadian Armed Forces reservists for training and deployment (incl. pre/post-deployment). Non-discretionary, available to all employees regardless of length of service; regular employees keep accruing seniority. Training leave capped at **20 days/calendar year** per the Reservists' Leave Regulation; 4 weeks' notice required. *(37.0X, PDF p.91)*
- **New leave-privacy guarantee** — Parties affirm employees' right to have leave-related personal info kept confidential. Effective **April 1, 2028**, the Employer must design timesheets/schedules/records so an employee's leave type isn't unnecessarily disclosed. *(37.00 preamble, PDF p.89)*
- **37.02 — faster, accountable leave decisions** — For unpaid-leave requests with **>21 days' notice**, the Employer must respond in writing **within 10 days**; and on request must give **written reasons** when a leave is denied. *(37.02, PDF p.89–90)*
- **37.03 — reservists' leave protects increments** — Increments are now also unaffected by reservists' leave (added to educational + parental leave). *(37.03, PDF p.91)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **37.04 Domestic & Sexual Violence Leave struck here** — The full DSVL clause is deleted from Article 37 but **relocated to Article 41** (handled separately); treat as a structural move, not a loss of entitlement. *(37.04, PDF p.90)*
