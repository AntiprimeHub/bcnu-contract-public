# MOA: Extended Work Day / Compressed Work Week — Gains & Losses

## ✅ Gains
- **27.05(D) new double-time trigger (EWD/CWW)** — Adds double time for hours worked **in excess of scheduled daily full-shift hours when worked after an extended-hours shift** under this MOA. New penalty pay that did not exist before; protects nurses pushed past a long extended shift. Effective **1st pp after Apr 1, 2027** (deferred). *(27.05(D), PDF p.134)*
- Note: 27.05(A)–(C) are unchanged context (transcribed for reference); the only operative change is the added (D). The "Employees will not be entitled to overtime under more than one of (a) or (b)" anti-pyramiding line in (B)(3)(b) is unchanged and correctly present.

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **27.05(D) interaction with 26.07** — The clause states it "does not supersede Article 26.07 – Paid End of Shift Work." So sub-15-minute / end-of-shift work still follows 26.07, not this new double-time rule. The new double-time appears to apply only to whole extra shift-length hours after an extended shift — confirm scope (no baseline in corpus to diff against). *(27.05(D), PDF p.134)*
