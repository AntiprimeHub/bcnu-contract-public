# ARTICLE 59 – Professional Responsibility Clause — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-59-professional-responsibility-clause.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-59-professional-responsibility-clause.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **PRC process becomes operational (by Nov 30, 2026)** — The old note saying this language was "not operational until ratification of the next Collective Agreement" is deleted. Replaced with a commitment that the parties start work within 60 days of ratification and that the full PRC/SRC professional-responsibility process becomes operative on or by November 30, 2026. The detailed problem-solving process (72-hour manager discussion, PRC within 14 days, SRC referral, binding unanimous recommendations) is unchanged and now activates. *(Art. 59 intro, PDF p.108)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Not live at ratification; subject to "modernize" rework** — The clause is not in force on day one. The new language concedes parts of the provision are "outdated" and the parties will "realign and modernize" it before the Nov 30, 2026 go-live. Net direction is a strengthening (it finally turns on), but the exact final wording is to be developed, so the operational details could shift. The substantive sub-clauses (59.01–59.07) themselves carry no tracked edits. *(Art. 59 intro, PDF p.108)*
