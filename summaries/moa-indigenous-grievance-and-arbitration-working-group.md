# MOA — Indigenous Grievance and Arbitration Working Group — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-indigenous-grievance-and-arbitration-working-group.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument, no baseline)

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gain · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 2 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ✅ Gains
- **Elder / Respected Community Member support in grievances** — Working Group (or sub-committee) will prioritize joint recommendations letting Indigenous employees request involvement of an Elder or respected community member in grievance procedures and other employer meetings. A concrete (if deferred) benefit aimed at a real entitlement; explicitly does not block local advance agreements to facilitate such attendance now. *(Elder/Respected Community Member Support section, PDF p.165)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Working Group convened by April 1, 2026** — HEABC convenes a Working Group to review grievance/arbitration processes and make provincial/sector-wide recommendations (distinctions-based, anti-racism, cultural safety, "speak-up" culture). A study/recommendation body — no direct nurse entitlement. *(Working Group establishment + mandate bullets, PDF p.164)*
- **Working Group composition & mechanics** — reps from health authorities, affiliates, HEABC, bargaining associations, SMEs, and ISAR Forum reps; meets quarterly; develops terms of reference; gathers data; reports recommendations to the Provincial ISAR Forum. Procedural. *(Working Group "will" bullets, PDF p.164)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Elder-support benefit is a recommendation, not an entitlement** — the Elder/community-member involvement is a commitment to *develop recommendations* by **March 31, 2029**; it is not itself an enforceable right, has no funding commitment stated, and may not be in place during the term. Watch the 2029 deadline. *(Elder/Respected Community Member Support section, PDF p.165)*
