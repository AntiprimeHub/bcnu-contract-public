# ARTICLE 49 – Pension Plan — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-49-pension-plan.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-49-pension-plan.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **Temp coverage** — Municipal Pension Plan coverage now explicitly extends to "employees in temporary appointments or temporary positions," who join the same plan as regular employees. *(49.01, PDF p.104)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Deleted 12-month temp clause** — The standalone rule that a temporary employee in continuous full-time work for 12 months "shall be enrolled in the Plan as a condition of employment" is struck. Read with the new inclusion language above this looks like consolidation, not a loss — but the explicit 12-month auto-enrollment trigger is gone, so confirm temp staff aren't worse off on timing of enrollment. *(49.01, PDF p.104)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **No contribution/benefit change** — No change to contribution rates, the 3-month join window, the part-time opt-out, or the casual-employee enrollment threshold. A footnote states the edits only reflect the parties' Sept 19, 2025 settlement and do not alter its terms. *(49.01, PDF p.104)*
