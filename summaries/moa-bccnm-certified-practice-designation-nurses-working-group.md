# MOA – BCCNM-Certified Practice Designation Nurses Working Group — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/moa-bccnm-certified-practice-designation-nurses-working-group.md` · Baseline: none (new instrument)

**Tally:** ✅ 0 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Study-only working group** — on or before **April 1, 2028**, Employer and Union each appoint two members to a working group to identify/gather data on BCCNM-certified practice nurses and "consider potential ways" to recognize those certifications. No pay, premium, or recognition commitment — exploratory only. *(sole clause)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **No binding outcome and a late start** — the group need not even form until April 1, 2028 (near the end of the agreement term), and the language is only to "consider" recognition. Members hoping for certification pay should not count on a tangible result within this contract. *(sole clause)*
