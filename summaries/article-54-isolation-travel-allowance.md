# ARTICLE 54 – Isolation Travel Allowance — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-54-isolation-travel-allowance.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-54-isolation-travel-allowance.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 1 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 1 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **8 new eligible communities (effective Apr 1, 2027)** — Boston Bar, Grand Forks/Kettle Valley, Hope, Lytton, Sointula, Texada Island, Ucluelet, and Zeballos added to the $150/month isolation travel allowance list. All eight are asterisked and become entitled effective April 1, 2027. *(Note: Daajing Giids also shows up in the tracked changes, but it was **already on the in-force list** — it was only relocated within the column layout, not newly added, and carries no asterisk.)* *(Art. 54, PDF p.105–106)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **No dollar increase** — The allowance stays at $150/month with no rate increase across the entire 2025–2029 term. The old "Effective April 1, 2023" trigger is replaced with plain "Employees," so the rate is unchanged from the prior agreement. *(Art. 54, PDF p.105)*

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **Modernization-review clause deleted** — The clause committing the parties to meet and develop list-eligibility principles before the next round of bargaining is struck (now superseded by the actual list expansion). *(Art. 54, PDF p.106)*
