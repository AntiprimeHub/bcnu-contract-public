# ARTICLE 56 – Payment of Wages — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/article-56-payment-of-wages.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/article-56-payment-of-wages.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 2 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 0 neutral · ⚠️ 0 watch-outs

## ✅ Gains
- **Retro pay deadline: 120 days** — New language requires Health Authorities and PHC to issue retroactive General Wage Increase payments to current employees within 120 days of ratification — a hard, enforceable deadline that didn't exist before. *(56.02, PDF p.107)*
- **Right to a retro-pay explanation** — On request, the Employer must provide a statement of wages (per Art. 56.04) explaining how an employee's retroactive payment was calculated, improving transparency and the ability to catch errors. *(56.02, PDF p.107)*
