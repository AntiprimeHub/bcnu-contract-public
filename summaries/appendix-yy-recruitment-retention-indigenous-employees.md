# Appendix YY — Recruitment & Retention of Indigenous Employees — Gains & Losses
Source: `changes/appendix-yy-recruitment-retention-indigenous-employees.md` · Baseline: `current/sections/appendix-yy-memorandum-of-agreement-recruitment-and-retention-of-indigen.md`

**Tally:** ✅ 0 gains · ❌ 0 losses · ➖ 4 neutral · ⚠️ 1 watch-out

## ➖ Neutral / Admin
- **New clause 6** — adds that the MOA "will form part of the Employers' and Unions' joint commitment to advance reconciliation and build a more equitable and culturally safe healthcare system." Aspirational framing language; no new concrete recruitment/retention entitlement. *(clause 6, PDF p.159)*
- **Clause 1 anti-racism sentence added** — new sentence: ongoing harms "are best addressed with concerted efforts to embed Indigenous-Specific Anti Racism." Statement of principle, no operative obligation. *(clause 1, PDF p.158)*
- **Clause 2 selection-criteria reword (distinctions-based)** — where multiple qualified Indigenous candidates exist, "give consideration to … the candidates' Indigenous knowledge and experience" is reworded to a "distinctions-based approach" weighing candidates' "relationships, knowledge and/or experience with or in the communities or populations being served." Restructured selection language; the existing employer ability to select an Indigenous candidate (clauses 2–4) is unchanged. *(clause 2, PDF p.158)*
- **Clause 2 community-engagement sentence added** — new: the Employer "may engage with the specific community or communities being served" in developing/applying these considerations. Permissive ("may"), no commitment. *(clause 2, PDF p.158-159)*

## ⚠️ Watch-outs
- **Selection reword — net effect ambiguous** — the reworded clause 2 still does not create a hiring entitlement for any individual nurse; it preserves the Employer's existing discretion to select an Indigenous candidate over a more-senior candidate in defined circumstances. Read as administrative refinement, but flagged because it touches seniority-based selection. No change to the 30-day return-to-prior-position safeguard or 28-day notice. *(clause 2, PDF p.158-159)*
