# BCNU 2025–2029 Settlement — What You GAIN / What You GIVE UP

**A plain-language guide for your ratification vote.** Every line below is sourced to the
settlement booklet (PDF page) and the clause it changes. This is built to be *honest first,
persuasive second* — the wins are real, but so are the give-ups and the fine print, and you
should see all of it before you vote.

*Independent and unofficial — not affiliated with BCNU/NBA, and not advice. **AI-assisted and
human-directed**, then cross-checked against the official documents (full method in
[METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md)). The whole project is public and version-tracked on GitHub —
every change is logged with a date and a reason, so nothing is edited quietly. Where this guide
and the official documents disagree, the official documents govern — verify anything that
matters before you vote.*

> **How to read this:** ✅ = a gain · ❌ = a loss / give-up · ➖ = housekeeping (no change to
> what you get) · ⚠️ = a catch worth knowing (a condition, a cap, or a gain that doesn't start
> until 2026/2027/2028). Term of the deal: **April 1, 2025 → March 31, 2029** (4 years).
> Most provisions take force **30 days after ratification** unless a later date is noted.

| | Count | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| ✅ **Gains** | **112** | New/larger pay, premiums, leave, benefits, protections. *(Note: ~16 of these are individual health-benefit line-item upgrades in one MOA — real, but small each.)* |
| ❌ **Losses / give-ups** | **9** | Removed or reduced entitlements, or new employer rights at your expense. |

| ⚠️ **Watch-outs** | **69** | Conditions, caps, time-limited pilots, and **deferred** gains that phase in 2026–2028. |

**The honest summary:** this is a gains-heavy deal led by guaranteed wage increases, richer
premiums, expanded leaves, and a major reshaping of the benefits plan. But a real chunk of the
headline money **doesn't arrive until 2027–2028**, the benefit plan moves to a **formula-funded
trust** instead of a fixed employer guarantee, the new **Shift-Select Positions** trade scheduling
protections for flexibility, and a handful of older MOAs and protections were **deleted or quietly
thinned**. Details below.

## 🏆 Top wins

1. **Guaranteed wage increases: 3% + 3% + 3% + 3%** — effective the first pay period after
   **Apr 1 2025, 2026, 2027, 2028** (~**12.5% compounded** over the term). 2025/2026 are
   retroactive on ratification; **2027 and 2028 are still in the future.**
   *(Art 63 A.1, PDF p.116)*
2. **$100M / $200M / $300M in new provincial staffing funding** for minimum nurse-to-patient
   ratios across **FY2026/27, FY2027/28, FY2028/29** (and ongoing). *(mNPR Funding MOA, cl.1 —*
   ⚠️ *DRAFT, paid to health authorities, not guaranteed dollar-for-dollar to nurses.)*
3. **Regular premium up ~67%: $2.15 → $3.00 (2026) → $3.20 (2027) → $3.60 (2028)**; evening
   premium rises too; **missed/recalled meal penalties double from 1.5× to 2×.**
   *(Art 28.05 / 26.03, PDF p.69–73 — all phase in 2026+.)*
4. **Health-benefit upgrades**: LTD earnings threshold **$5,843 → $8,318 (RN/RPN) / $5,110
   (LPN)**, threshold now re-indexed every 2 years, plus a slate of plan improvements; backed by
   a **$175M NBA seed contribution** into a new union-influenced benefits trust.
   *(H&W Benefits MOA, PDF p.179–187 — effective Apr 1 2027, contingent on a Trust Agreement.)*
5. **New and bigger leaves**: Indigenous Child Care Leave **up to 62 weeks** (Apr 2026); new
   **Reservists' Leave**; paid compassionate leave **5 → 6 days**; a citizenship-ceremony day
   (Apr 2027). *(Arts 47, 37, 33, 43)*
6. **Casual Hours Incentive: up to $2,430/yr** for high-hours casuals ($1,000 at ≥600 hrs).
   *(Art 11.04(L), PDF p.54 — starts Jan 1 2027.)*
7. **Stronger safety law**: new enforceable duties on exposure to drugs/biohazards, plus new
   **sexual & domestic violence** and **violence-risk-mitigation** protections.
   *(Art 32.03(D)/32.07/32.08, PDF p.79–84)*
8. **Anti-agency-nurse protections** (your shifts offered first; agency nurses can't supervise
   you), **accommodation-first duty** on the Employer, and **layoff protection extended to AI /
   tech changes.** *(Art 6.02, App A.6, Art 20.01)*
9. **Overtime by seniority** for all pre-booked OT >48h out (precondition removed); **+1
   full-time steward** (Comox & Campbell River); **EDMP disability reps 22 → 24.**
   *(Art 27.07, App W, App A.2)*

## ⚠️ What you give up (and the big catches)

1. **Hidden reduction — Red-Circled wage protection.** When Appendix EE was folded into the
   articles, the **Classification Transition / Red-Circled** rule was cut down to a vague
   2-sentence paraphrase in Article 62 — dropping the "Green-Circled" status, the **March 31 2019**
   protection date, the paid-at-higher-level mechanics, the forfeiture conditions, and "increment
   progression continues." Presented as "housekeeping." *(App EE §3.b → Art 62, PDF p.145/148)*
2. **Deleted MOAs.** Six appendices struck — most are spent, but **Appendix Q** (path for
   home-support nurses to convert to **regular status**) and **Appendix JJ.2** (a **specialty-
   education funding floor**) carried live value with no shown replacement. *(General Housekeeping
   MOA, PDF p.155–209)*
3. **Shift-Select Positions trade protections for flexibility.** SSP nurses pick shifts by
   seniority, but **most of Article 25's scheduling protections are switched off**, SSP shifts
   earn **no overtime**, peak-period hours can be **mandatory**, and the new position type **risks
   eroding fixed lines.** *(Art 17, PDF p.59–60)*
4. **Benefits become a formula-funded trust.** Article 60's fixed, 100%-employer-paid guarantees
   are replaced by an **Employer-Funded Nurses' Benefits Trust** funded by a %-of-payroll formula;
   **trustees can amend the plan**, and **massage is newly capped at $4,000.** Union gets more
   control; the employer's open-ended cost guarantee goes away. *(H&W MOA + EFNBT formula, p.179–197)*
5. **mNPR: a legal give-up.** In exchange for the staffing funding, the **NBA waives all disputes**
   over the Ministry's compliance with the 2023 ratios MOU up to now. *(mNPR MOA, cl.6)*
6. **Small cuts**: union bulletin-board print ratio cut (Art 6.08); **isolation travel allowance
   frozen at $150/mo** for the whole 4-year term (Art 54).
7. **A lot of the money is deferred.** None of the premium-dollar increases hit at ratification;
   wage years 3–4 and most benefit upgrades land in **2027/2028** (see fine print).

## ⚠️ Watch-outs & fine print

**Two unmarked "silent edits" in the booklet** (the change wasn't underlined, so a reader
trusting the markup would miss it — both confirmed against the in-force contract):
- **Art 16.03(A):** the words **"by the Employer"** were added, unmarked, to who removes/destroys
  disciplinary records after 18 months. Likely benign, but slipped in untracked. *(PDF p.57)*
- **Appendix XX:** a capitalized **"The"** was inserted, unmarked, to restart a sentence. Cosmetic.
  *(PDF p.156)*

**Restatements that are NOT cuts (don't be misled either way):**
- **Indigenous ceremonial leave "5 days → 37.5 hours"** (Art 46.02) is the *same* entitlement —
  1 day = 7.5h, so 5 days = 37.5h. FTE-neutral. *(Compressed-week staff should confirm.)*
- Large strike-throughs in **Appendix EE** are mostly **verbatim relocations** into Arts 6/11/13/18
  — no loss there (the real loss is the Red-Circled reduction, give-up item #1 above).
- **COLA (Appendix VV) deletion is NOT a forward-looking loss.** The Cost-of-Living Adjustment
  only ever applied to April 2023 and April 2024 — it was already spent. Deleting it in 2025
  removes nothing you'd have received over 2025–2029. *(App VV, PDF p.154–155; confirmed obsolete.)*
- **Gender-affirming medical leave (8 weeks paid) is NOT new.** It was already in the in-force
  contract (effective **April 1, 2023**, Appendix AAA). The only thing this round changes in that
  appendix is a **180-day deadline for inclusive bathroom/changeroom signage** — so don't read the
  8-week leave as a 2025–2029 win. *(App AAA, PDF p.162–163.)*

**Gains that DON'T start at ratification (deferred):**
- Wage increases **Year 3 (Apr 1 2027)** and **Year 4 (Apr 1 2028)**.
- **All premium-dollar increases** (Art 28) — first ones Apr 1 2026.
- Meal-penalty 2× rate (Art 26.03) — Apr 1 2026.
- **Health-benefit upgrades + LTD changes** — Apr 1 2027, *contingent on a Trust Agreement by
  Jul 1 2026* (Vince Ready mediation if not reached).
- **Casual Hours Incentive** — Jan 1 2027.
- **Indigenous Child Care Leave** — Apr 1 2026. **Citizenship-ceremony day** — Apr 1 2027.
- **EDMP reps 22→24** and **new isolation-travel communities** — Apr 1 2027.
- **Professional Responsibility Clause** goes operative — by **Nov 30 2026** (wording still TBD).
- **Shift-Select Positions** — no stated effective date beyond ratification.

**Time-limited pilots (end ~2029, no rollout guaranteed):**
- **Weapons-screening pilot** — 4 sites only, mostly voluntary, $3.73M funded, report-only.
- **Regional Joint OHS Committees pilot** — 2 years, continuation self-assessed.
- **BCHOA Rapid Dispute Resolution** — trial to **Mar 30 2029**; decisions set no precedent and
  use a stripped-down process (10-min submissions, no witnesses).

**"Working groups / committees" with no direct entitlement** (study bodies, may not deliver in
the term): Indigenous Grievance & Arbitration WG, Indigenous Workforce Committee, DEI WG,
**BCCNM Certified Practice WG** (need not even form until Apr 1 2028).
